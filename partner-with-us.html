Who our audience is

The WICG reader is a professional woman aged 35 to 65. She has investable assets ranging from $10,000 to several million. She is making her own financial decisions, often for the first time, and she is taking those decisions seriously. She is skeptical of crypto's bro culture, allergic to FOMO marketing, and looking for trustworthy education and analysis to make informed decisions. She tends to gravitate toward conservative, fundamentals-backed assets: real-world assets, stablecoins, tokenized treasuries, regulated platforms, and yield-bearing strategies with clear risk profiles.

She is also looking for community. She wants other women asking the same questions she is asking, sharing what they have learned, and building wealth alongside her. The WICG Facebook group and upcoming premium community tier exist for that exact need.

Who we will not work with

We are selective about partnerships because our audience is selective about who they trust. We will not work with:

  • Memecoin projects. No exceptions.
  • Unregulated offshore exchanges or platforms with a history of compliance issues.
  • "Get rich quick" or "guaranteed returns" products. If the marketing language wouldn't pass SEC scrutiny, we won't promote it.
  • Multi-level marketing structures dressed as investment opportunities.
  • Token launches where the primary purpose is price speculation.
  • Brands that have shown bad faith toward women through their marketing, leadership, or culture.

This list is not exhaustive. We use editorial judgment. If you are unsure whether your project is a fit, please reach out and we will tell you honestly.

Our commitment to our readers

Sponsorships will never compromise our editorial integrity. Sponsored content is always labeled. Sponsors do not get editorial coverage in exchange for paying us. The wall between editorial and advertising is absolute. We have built this publication on trust, and we will not trade that trust for short-term revenue.

If we ever fall short of this commitment, please tell us. We will correct, disclose, and improve. The publication exists to serve readers first. Sponsors are welcome partners, but readers are the priority.
