About WICG · Founded 2021

Crypto is being rewritten. Women are holding the pen.

Women in Crypto Global is the leading independent publication for the 44% of new crypto wallet holders who are women. We exist to make sure that, this time, the rewrite includes us.

For most of modern financial history, women were either excluded from the conversation entirely or addressed as an afterthought. Banking, investing, real estate, retirement planning. The infrastructure, the language, the marketing, the boardrooms. All of it was built by men, for men, with women as a secondary market to be educated into accepting the system as it stood. When crypto emerged in the late 2010s, it threatened to repeat the same pattern. Early crypto media was written for men, by men, with a vernacular and tone that actively pushed women away. We started Women in Crypto Global in 2021 to change that.

Today, the data is clear. Women are not crypto's audience. We are crypto's growth story. The largest cohort of new wallet holders in 2026 is women aged 45 to 59. The fastest-growing demographic of crypto investors globally is women. The category of crypto that aligns most closely with how women prefer to invest (real-world assets, stablecoin yield, regulated platforms) is the fastest-growing category in the entire space. The financial system is being rewritten in real time, and for the first time in history, women are arriving at the table early.

What we are

WICG is an independent media publication. We report on crypto, blockchain, and Web3 with a specific editorial focus on the topics that matter most for women building wealth: real-world assets, stablecoin yield, tokenized real estate, regulation, security, and the news that genuinely changes how women should think about money. We cover the daily market when it matters and skip it when it doesn't. We write in plain English. We assume our readers are intelligent adults who do not need to be condescended to.

We are also a community. The WICG Facebook group, founded in 2021, is home to over 8,000 women learning crypto together. The site you are reading is the publication arm. The newsletter (Tokenized Report, published Sundays) is the digest. A premium community tier launches in summer 2026 with deeper resources, member-only research, and monthly office hours.

What we are not

We are not a course or a coaching program. We are not a token project. We do not take affiliate compensation from exchanges or protocols we cover. We are not a Twitter influencer brand. We do not promise you 100x returns, generational wealth, or financial freedom. We do not believe in shortcuts, get-rich-quick narratives, or any version of crypto culture that treats serious investment decisions as entertainment.

We are not a publication that addresses women as if we are different from other readers. We do not write "crypto for moms" or "the girl's guide to blockchain." We assume our readers are professional women, sophisticated investors, careful learners, and capable adults who happen to want media built for the way they actually live and invest. That is the only difference.

We do not write crypto for women. We write crypto, and we happen to do it without the bro energy that makes most crypto media unreadable.

Our editorial principles

Six principles guide everything we publish:

  • Independence first. We do not accept compensation, equity, tokens, or any other consideration from companies we cover. Sponsorships and partnerships, when they exist, are clearly labeled as such and held to journalistic standards.
  • Plain English. Jargon is laziness. If we use a technical term, we define it. If we cannot explain something clearly, we do not write about it.
  • Real research. Every cornerstone article is built on primary sources, public data, and named experts. We do not regurgitate Twitter takes.
  • No price predictions. We do not tell readers when Bitcoin will hit $100K or which altcoin will moon. Predictions belong to influencers. Analysis belongs to us.
  • Education over urgency. Crypto media weaponizes FOMO. We refuse to. If a story is genuinely urgent, we cover it as such. Otherwise, we treat readers as long-term investors making considered decisions.
  • Women as the default audience. Every editorial decision assumes a woman is the reader. Not "men plus women." Not "general audience that happens to include women." Women, first and foremost.

You can read our full editorial standards at womenincryptoglobal.com/editorial-standards.

The woman behind it

WICG was founded and is currently led by Julie James, a long-time community builder and crypto investor who started the original Facebook group in 2021 when she realized there was no media publication that addressed women in crypto the way the audience actually needed to be addressed. The publication you are reading was built independently, on a lean budget, with the goal of becoming the trusted media brand for women in the next generation of finance.

If you want to reach Julie directly, the best path is the newsletter (she replies personally to subscriber replies for the first year) or the contact form at womenincryptoglobal.com/contact.

Where we are going

The next three years are the most important time in the history of women in financial markets. The shift from analog finance to on-chain finance is real, and it is irreversible. Women who enter this category with good information will compound that advantage for decades. Women who stay on the sidelines because they do not have a publication that speaks their language will miss it.

Our goal is not to be the biggest crypto publication. Our goal is to be the publication that women trust most. Five years from now, we want WICG to be cited the way the Wall Street Journal is cited, recognized the way Morning Brew is recognized, and trusted the way your sharpest friend is trusted. We are building toward that, one article, one newsletter, one community conversation at a time.

Thank you for being here. The work matters because you are here.
